Photoset 002 is a cohesive soft-window-light lifestyle editorial series. The entire atmosphere is soft, gray, pale, quiet, and emotionally restrained.

The subject is an adult-presenting woman photographed in a relaxed home outfit. The key outfit pieces are an oversized light gray zip hoodie worn loose and slipping off the shoulders, a pale gray or white camisole with thin straps, black fitted shorts, small delicate earrings, and a minimal necklace.
