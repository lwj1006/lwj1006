# Photoset 019 - Dark Grotto Pink Tulle Hydrangea Lily Portrait

**Language:** English
**Purpose:** reusable photoset prompt template for anime image generation.
**Reference rule:** reference photos define composition, pose, wardrobe language, lighting, props, and atmosphere only. They do not define the final character identity.

---

## 0. Global Photoset DNA

**Core identity:** dark gray floral grotto, pink tulle dress, hydrangea and lily flowers, misty dramatic beam light, romantic cave-like flower chamber.

**Wardrobe system:** soft pink tulle or gauze dress with layered skirt, delicate straps or ruffles, floral feminine styling and pale translucent fabric.

**Scene system:** dark gray grotto-like flower room with hydrangeas, lilies, mossy textures, mist, stone-gray background and dense floral foreground.

**Lighting and color:** dreamy mist beam light, soft highlights cutting through darker floral space, pink fabric glow, low contrast face fill and atmospheric haze.

**Emotional tone:** mysterious romantic flower grotto, quiet fantasy portrait, dreamy and slightly melancholic.

**Continuity rule:** keep the same wardrobe family, prop language, lighting palette, and room atmosphere across the set while changing pose, crop, and camera distance for each image.
