## Global Visual Identity of Photoset 010

Photoset 010 is an outdoor daylight portrait series built around a simple but extremely strong color formula: **bright red cardigan + white dress + pale skin tones + blue sky + urban railings/buildings**. The outfit is casual, fresh, and instantly readable. The subject wears a vivid red ribbed knit cardigan over a white dress or white camisole-style top with a short flared skirt. The red cardigan becomes the visual anchor in every image. The white dress/skirt creates movement, lightness, and contrast. Round metal-frame glasses and a short brown bob haircut give the character a friendly, approachable, slightly bookish identity.

The set is not a studio fashion series. It is built from real outdoor locations: a rooftop terrace with railings and blurred buildings, a Japanese street with a large blue right-turn traffic sign, and a waterfront/amusement area with a large Ferris wheel and concrete steps. The images have a travel snapshot / casual portrait / urban day-off feeling, but the photography is still composed and editorial. It is bright, clean, high-key, and wind-sensitive.

The main character identity anchors are:
1. adult woman with short brown bob haircut;
2. round wire-frame glasses;
3. vivid red ribbed cardigan, open front, small buttons;
4. white sleeveless dress or camisole dress with short flared skirt;
5. white or pale low-heeled sandals/slides;
6. bright sunlight, blue sky, city background;
7. soft smiling expression;
8. breezy hair and skirt movement;
9. rooftop railing, street sign, or Ferris wheel environment;
10. playful, wholesome, urban summer mood.

The mood is cheerful and light. The subject smiles often, looks directly at the camera, and uses simple poses: leaning forward with hands on knees, sitting on a rooftop floor with knees bent, shielding the sun with one hand, pointing playfully at a traffic sign, balancing with one leg raised, or sitting on steps in front of a Ferris wheel. The poses are dynamic without becoming athletic. They should feel like a casual photo walk on a clear sunny day.

Lighting is strong midday or early afternoon sun. Shadows are crisp, especially on the rooftop floor and street pavement. Highlights are bright and sometimes overexposed on the white dress and legs. The red cardigan holds saturated color. The background often uses shallow depth of field, leaving buildings soft and geometric. Some images use a slight Dutch angle or tilted horizon, adding spontaneous energy.

For prompt engineering, this set is valuable because it teaches how to combine:
- **strong color blocking**: red cardigan against white dress and blue sky;
- **casual city portrait styling**: rooftop, railing, road sign, city buildings;
- **wind movement**: hair and skirt lift;
- **friendly character design**: glasses, bob haircut, smile;
- **simple readable poses**: leaning, sitting, pointing, shielding eyes;
- **bright high-key daylight**: hard shadows, clean whites, crisp outdoor atmosphere.

The series should not become a fantasy image, school uniform image, idol stage image, beach image, or formal fashion shoot. It should remain a sunny urban day-off portrait with cheerful casual clothing and natural city locations.

# AI Reproduction Strategy

## 1. Most Important Anchors

To preserve Photoset 010 identity, prioritize:

1. vivid red ribbed cardigan;

3. round wire-frame glasses;
4. short brown bob haircut;
5. bright urban outdoor daylight;

If the cardigan changes color, the set loses its identity. If the glasses disappear, the subject becomes generic. If the bob becomes long hair, the character identity collapses. If the location becomes indoor or nighttime, the entire mood changes.

## 2. What Can Be Simplified

The exact building shapes, exact railing color, exact Ferris wheel gondola count, exact road sign position, exact sandal heel shape, and exact background architecture can vary. The core identity is **red cardigan + white dress + glasses + short bob + sunny city photo walk**.

## 3. What Must Not Be Lost

Do not lose:
- red cardigan;
- white dress/skirt;
- round glasses;
- short bob;
- bright sunlight;
- city setting;
- cheerful casual expression.

## 4. Difficulty Ranking

From easiest to hardest:

1. Image 5 — standing under road sign with one leg lifted;
2. Image 1 — rooftop forward lean;
3. Image 2 — seated rooftop side pose;
4. Image 6 — seated Ferris wheel steps portrait;

Image 4 is hardest because it requires accurate sign placement, full-body anatomy, two pointing arms, and clear interaction with the arrow sign.
