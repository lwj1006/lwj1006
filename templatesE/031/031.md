## Global Set Identity

This seventh set is a romantic indoor fantasy editorial built around a **large sunlit window**, a **dense pastel floral wall**, and a dramatic **blue soft draped neckline gown**. Compared with the previous pink-tulle fig-table set, this one is more princess-like, painterly, and theatrical. The subject is styled almost like a floral fairy, a spring princess, or a romantic storybook heroine placed inside a curated atelier.

The strongest identity anchors are:

- **Large wooden window frame**
- **Extremely bright backlight from the window**
- **Cream draped curtain fabric**
- **Dense pastel flower wall behind the window**
- **Hydrangea-like blue flowers, pale pink roses, yellow blossoms, white flowers**
- **Blue soft draped neckline gown with voluminous sleeves and long train**
- **Bare feet**
- **Long dark hair with floral hair ornaments**

- **Lace-covered side table**
- **White classical pedestal vase**
- **Painting on easel**

The set has a clear **romantic storybook / painter’s studio / floral conservatory** feeling. It is not a realistic casual room; it is a controlled editorial fantasy space designed around window light and flower abundance.
