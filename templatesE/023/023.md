# Photoset 023 - Modern Home Gray Modest Top Casual Portrait

**Language:** English
**Purpose:** reusable photoset prompt template for anime image generation.
**Reference rule:** reference photos define composition, pose, wardrobe language, lighting, props, and atmosphere only. They do not define the final character identity.

---

## 0. Global Photoset DNA

**Core identity:** modern home, gray ribbed modest sleeveless top, modest light blue denim shorts, sofa, white wall, green plants, rattan chair, clear natural daylight and relaxed casual portrait.

**Wardrobe system:** light gray ribbed modest sleeveless top with secure shoulder coverage, light blue high-waist denim shorts, minimal gold bracelet, simple casual footwear or socks, clean modern casual styling with conservative coverage.

**Scene system:** modern low-saturation home interior with gray fabric sofa, white wall, black-frame window, wooden geometric screen, large green plants, rattan chair and clean floor.

**Lighting and color:** clear natural window light, soft shadows, low-saturation gray-white palette, warm skin and hair accents, airy realistic room depth but anime illustration finish.

**Emotional tone:** clean relaxed home portrait, natural, approachable, modern and lightly mature.

**Continuity rule:** keep the same wardrobe family, prop language, lighting palette, and room atmosphere across the set while changing pose, crop, and camera distance for each image.
