# Photoset 020 - Peach Minimal Dried Lotus Ikebana Portrait

**Language:** English
**Purpose:** reusable photoset prompt template for anime image generation.
**Reference rule:** reference photos define composition, pose, wardrobe language, lighting, props, and atmosphere only. They do not define the final character identity.

---

## 0. Global Photoset DNA

**Core identity:** peach-pink minimal studio, dried lotus ikebana, beige gauze outfit, oriental still-life portrait, restrained soft elegance.

**Wardrobe system:** warm beige or peach gauze dress with soft drape, translucent but tasteful fabric layers, simple elegant silhouette and minimal accessories.

**Scene system:** minimal peach or cream studio with dried lotus pods, ikebana branches, sparse vase arrangement, pale floor and large quiet negative space.

**Lighting and color:** soft peach studio light, clean high-key exposure, gentle shadows under dried stems, warm skin highlights and low visual noise.

**Emotional tone:** quiet oriental still-life portrait, poetic, minimal, warm and contemplative.

**Continuity rule:** keep the same wardrobe family, prop language, lighting palette, and room atmosphere across the set while changing pose, crop, and camera distance for each image.
