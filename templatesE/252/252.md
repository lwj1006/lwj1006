# Template 252 - Airy Floral Balcony Lounge Set

Preserve the exact curtain overlap, furniture support, prop placement, and crop of each shot while simplifying distant greenery.

The outfit is a coordinated, fully opaque two-piece summer lounge set, not lingerie. Use lightweight white cotton voile printed with tiny scattered dusty-pink and muted lavender flower sprigs. The cropped peasant top has a wide square-to-off-shoulder elastic neckline, short gathered puff sleeves, a loose blouson bodice, lilac piping, narrow ruffled trim, a tiny lilac center bow, and a gathered elastic hem ending above the natural waist. The matching high-rise bloomer shorts have a broad gathered elastic waistband, voluminous but opaque short legs, lilac piping and tiny ruffles at both leg openings, and small lilac side bows. Add an opaque white lining so no skin or underwear shows through the floral fabric. Bare feet are used in the wider shots. Pink organza bows visible in the photoset person's hair are optional costume accents only; never replace the selected character's canonical hairstyle or fixed head accessories.

Use soft overexposed daylight from the curtain side, mainly frame left or front-left, with cool green window fill, pale peach skin shadows, low contrast, and a white, blush, lilac, honey-wood, and fresh-green palette.
