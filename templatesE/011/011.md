## Global Visual Identity of Photoset 011

Photoset 011 is an indoor bedroom portrait series built around the contrast between **cool blue-gray ambient shadow** and **warm directional window sunlight**. The visual identity is quieter, more cinematic, and more emotionally inward than the previous bright city sets. Instead of cheerful street motion, this series focuses on stillness, softness, and private interior atmosphere.

The subject is an adult woman with a short black bob haircut, soft bangs, clear facial features, and small visible beauty marks near the cheek / lower face area. Her styling is minimal but precise: a loose silver-gray satin shirt, partly slipped off one shoulder, worn over a white camisole with thin straps; delicate gold bracelets, thin rings, and a fine necklace. The metallic satin fabric catches light in ripples and folds, becoming one of the strongest visual textures in the set. The white camisole strap and pale bedding add softness, while the silver shirt adds cool reflective structure.

The room is a modern bedroom or soft lounge-bedroom space. Background elements include a cream faux-fur blanket, white pillows, blurred sofa or bed cushions, a floor lamp, blue-gray wall panels, white bedding, pale curtains, and soft furniture. Most backgrounds are intentionally out of focus, creating depth while keeping the face, hair, fabric texture, hands, and sunlight patches as the primary focus.

The lighting is the most important technical element. This set uses a highly recognizable light pattern:
1. cool blue-gray ambient room tone;
2. warm narrow sunlight strip falling across the face, neck, shoulder, hand, or thigh;
3. strong contrast between lit skin and shadowed fabric;
4. shallow depth of field;
5. soft bedroom diffusion;
6. cinematic low-key/high-key mixture.

The mood is contemplative and personal. The subject often sits or lies on the bed with knees drawn up, head resting on arm, hand near lips, hands raised toward window light, or hands gently holding the shirt near the chest. The poses are quiet and intimate but should remain elegant and non-explicit. The best prompt framing is **soft editorial bedroom portrait**, **cinematic morning light**, **quiet reflective mood**, and **delicate fabric detail**.

The series has five major pose types:
- Image 1: leaning against a cream faux-fur bed edge, head resting on arms, downward gaze;
- Image 2: seated side pose on bed with knees raised, looking directly at camera;
- Image 3: close-up face and hands, hand near lips, warm sun strip across face;
- Image 4: lying side profile on white bedding, hands lifted into sunlight;
- Image 5: seated side pose with knees raised, hands holding shirt near collar/chest, contemplative side gaze.

For AI prompting, the repeatable essence is:

```text
cool cinematic bedroom portrait, short black bob, silver-gray satin shirt slipped off one shoulder, white camisole strap, cream faux-fur blanket, blue-gray room, warm strip of window sunlight across face and shoulder, shallow depth of field, quiet intimate editorial mood
```
