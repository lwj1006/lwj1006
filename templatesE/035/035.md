# Detailed Image Analysis for Prompt Building  
## Set: Green Grape Floral Garden Room / Soft Botanical Sunlight

This document analyzes each image separately for prompt-building purposes. The focus is on clothing, scene design, lighting, pose/action, narrative mood, and reusable prompt keywords. The set has a unified visual language: pale floral chiffon, green grapes, hydrangea-like blue-white flowers, leafy greenhouse background, wooden platform, soft window light, and a fresh spring/summer botanical atmosphere.

---

## Global Set Identity

**Core visual concept:**  
A soft, greenhouse-inspired indoor garden portrait set built around pale green grapes, airy floral chiffon, white window frames, hydrangea clusters, fern leaves, tropical greenery, and clean daylight. The overall mood is gentle, fresh, delicate, and slightly dreamy.

**Color palette:**  
Cream white, pale sage green, fresh grape green, hydrangea blue, soft mint, warm honey wood, pale beige, and subtle blush pink skin tones.

**Key styling elements:**  
- Light floral chiffon two-piece / robe-like wrap outfit
- Soft translucent sleeves and tied bow details
- Pale lace inner camisole or bralette-like top
- Loose low updo with soft curled tendrils
- White-green flower hair accessories
- Long pale manicure
- Green grapes used as repeated prop
- Green apple used as secondary prop in several images
- Wooden floor/platform and green translucent tulle fabric
- Dense botanical background with white window-grid structure

**Lighting identity:**  
Bright but soft studio daylight, often filtered through leaves and window frames. Some images use stronger top/front light, creating clean highlights on skin and grapes. Shadows are gentle, with dappled botanical patterns in several frames.

**Recommended global prompt foundation:**  
`soft botanical indoor garden portrait, pale floral chiffon outfit, translucent fabric, green grapes, hydrangea flowers, white window frame, lush tropical leaves, wooden platform, fresh spring daylight, airy romantic atmosphere, soft high-key lighting, delicate feminine styling, shallow depth of field, editorial portrait photography`

# Image 1

## 1. Clothing
The outfit is a pale ivory and soft sage floral chiffon ensemble. It has thin white straps underneath and a translucent outer layer tied at the front with ribbon-like fabric. The sleeves are loose, sheer, and slightly puffed, giving the outfit a soft romantic garden-dress feeling. The fabric pattern is very subtle: small beige-green floral motifs scattered across a cream base. The neckline is delicate, with lace texture visible around the inner top. The styling feels light, fragile, and springlike rather than formal.

## 2. Scene
The subject stands or sits close to a white-framed greenhouse-style window. Behind her are dense green leaves, fern-like plants, and clusters of blue and white hydrangea flowers. Green grapes are placed in the lower-right foreground, partly cropped, acting as a color anchor. The background is full but not chaotic because the white window grid organizes the composition.

## 3. Light
The lighting is bright, clean, and cool-neutral. It resembles direct studio daylight softened through the greenhouse setup. Highlights appear on the face, shoulders, hands, and grapes. The skin is luminous, with a polished beauty-photography finish. The shadows are minimal, preserving a fresh, high-key atmosphere.

## 4. Action / Pose
The subject faces slightly downward with both hands held close to the upper chest, gently touching or holding a small green grape. Her head tilts down, eyes lowered, giving a shy and introspective expression. The hands create a soft triangular frame in front of the chest. The pose is compact and delicate.

## 5. Narrative
This image feels like a quiet moment in a greenhouse where the subject pauses before tasting a grape. The expression suggests tenderness, hesitation, and private thought. It is less playful than the rest of the set and works well as an opening portrait.

## 6. Prompt Notes
`close medium portrait, young woman in pale floral chiffon wrap outfit, white lace camisole, soft loose updo with white flower hairpin, holding small green grape near chest, looking downward, white greenhouse window behind, blue hydrangeas and lush green leaves, green grapes foreground, fresh high-key daylight, delicate shy mood, soft editorial beauty photography`
