## Global Set Identity

This set is visually different from the previous pastel domestic and outdoor garden sets. It is a **warm, golden, flower-filled indoor editorial** built around a soft bridal / vintage-romantic atmosphere.

The dominant visual language is:

- **Warm amber sunlight**
- **Wooden floor and wooden furniture**
- **White light layered dress**
- **Floral crown**
- **Short black bob haircut**
- **Soft lace and airy layered fabric**
- **Abundant flowers arranged around the subject**
- **Dreamlike film-style glow**
- **Quiet, melancholic, poetic portrait mood**

The photoset feels like a staged indoor floral room near a window during late afternoon. The light is intense and golden, producing strong warm highlights and deep orange shadows. The subject often appears seated low on the floor, wrapped in airy layered white lace and surrounded by flowers, giving the entire sequence a **romantic bridal dream / soft fairytale interior** quality.

# Set-Level Visual Diagnosis

The emotional success of this photoset comes from the contrast between **warm wooden realism** and **soft white floral fantasy**. The room feels tangible because of the wooden floors, furniture, cups, vases, and sun patches. The subject feels dreamlike because of the light layered dress, floral crown, soft haze, and romantic posture.

For AI prompting, the most important details to preserve are:

1. **Short black bob with blunt bangs**
2. **White floral crown**
3. **Light Layered white soft draped neckline lace dress**
4. **Warm orange wooden floor**
5. **Golden-hour indoor sunlight**
6. **Many pastel flowers in vases**
7. **Soft cinematic haze**
8. **Quiet melancholic romantic expression**
9. **Seated or reclining floor-level poses**
10. **Vintage flower-room atmosphere**
