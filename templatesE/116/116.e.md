# Template 116 — E-only inspected shot prompts

# Image 1 - Crossed knee and upright brush

## 1. Ready-to-Use Prompt

Reproduce this single shot. Vertical seated portrait from near eye level, head to lower shins, with the easel filling screen-left and the sitter centered to the right. Sit upright on a worn white square stool, cross one raised knee over the other leg and let both lower legs descend toward the bottom crop. Tilt the head slightly toward screen-left, meet the viewer with a small closed-mouth smile. Bend the screen-left arm and delicately hold a narrow brush upright beside the canvas; extend the opposite arm downward and brace the fingers on the stool at screen-right. Wear a white cotton cropped tank with a scoop neckline and broad straps, charcoal denim shorts with folded hems, and a pale beige fine-striped shirt tied at the waist with loose ends over the lap. Both tank straps remain on the shoulders. Keep the white canvas with teal, green, navy, black and small ochre-red strokes, wooden easel rails and a stained palette on its lower shelf. White window frames and sheer curtains enclose sunlit greenery behind, with paint supplies and books at the right sill. Diffused window daylight comes from behind and image-left with soft room fill across the face and torso, warm pale interior surfaces and cool green-blue paint accents.

# Image 2 - Two raised knees and mixing palette

## 1. Ready-to-Use Prompt

Reproduce this single shot. Vertical close seated composition from head to raised knees, with the knees filling the lower foreground and the easel behind at screen-left. Fold both legs toward the torso and turn the shoulders slightly toward screen-left. Look a little left of the viewer with a gentle closed-mouth smile. Bend both elbows: the screen-left hand holds a pink-handled brush angled down toward a clear oval palette, while the screen-right hand supports that palette horizontally at chest height. Keep the palette below the chin and the fingers distinct. Wear the white scoop-neck cotton crop tank with its screen-right strap slipped onto the upper arm, charcoal denim shorts and the beige pinstriped shirt loosely draped around both forearms and the lap; the opaque bodice remains secure. Keep the large white-framed window and green trees behind, the abstract blue-green canvas left and a book with scattered brushes on the sill right. Bright diffused window backlight outlines the shoulders, with gentle frontal room fill and restrained soft shadows beneath the hands and palette.

# Image 3 - Sideways bench seat and folded hands

## 1. Ready-to-Use Prompt

Reproduce this single shot. Vertical nearly full seated view at torso height, leaving space around the crossed lower legs and pale open-backed heels near the bottom edge. Sit sideways on a broad worn white bench with a splayed wooden support, thighs extending toward screen-left, knees together and ankles crossing below. Turn the shoulders and face back toward the viewer, gently tilt the head toward screen-left and part the mouth slightly. Bring both forearms down in front; rest the hands loosely together on the nearer upper thigh. Wear a white broad-strap scoop-neck tank, charcoal cuffed denim shorts and an open pale beige pinstriped shirt: one shoulder remains covered while the screen-right side slips to the upper arm, with long sleeves and wide cuffs draping around the wrists. Keep a large grid window and green lawn behind, a sliver of easel and open paint box at screen-left, stacked books at upper right and an open magazine on the bench at screen-right. Window light gives a cool gentle rim and diffuse front fill, with a soft bench contact shadow and muted white, gray, beige and green palette.

# Image 6 - Brush held at mouth and hands between knees

## 1. Ready-to-Use Prompt

Reproduce this single shot. Vertical frontal seated view from head to cropped knees. Sit on the stool with both knees bent outward toward the lower corners, lean the torso slightly forward, and bring both arms down between the knees with hands meeting at the bottom edge. Look directly toward the viewer with the mouth slightly open around the clean handle tip of one pink-handled brush; the gold ferrule and dark bristles hang diagonally down toward screen-left across the chest. Do not add a hand holding the brush or a palette. Wear the white cotton scoop-neck cropped tank with both broad straps upright, dark charcoal shorts and a pale beige pinstriped shirt knotted at the waist, its sleeves crossing the lap. Keep the close white canvas of blue-green abstract strokes and wooden easel rail at screen-left, a sheer curtain and white window frame behind, and stacked books on a wooden surface at screen-right. Soft daylight arrives from upper left and behind, with gentle room fill on the face and torso and small consistent shadows beneath the chin, brush and garment folds.
