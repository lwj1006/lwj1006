## Global Set Identity

This photoset is a **bright summer balcony / garden editorial** built around a leafy fig tree, intense natural daylight, white-green floral clothing, watermelon, green grapes, wicker baskets, and sun-dappled skin.

The visual identity is much fresher and more saturated than the previous warm bridal flower-room set. Instead of amber nostalgia, this set uses:

- vivid green foliage,
- strong summer sunlight,
- white floral fabric,
- red watermelon contrast,
- fresh fruit props,
- fig leaves and branches as framing devices,
- shallow depth of field,
- outdoor / balcony garden atmosphere,
- relaxed but polished portrait posing.

The set feels like a **summer fruit garden fantasy**, somewhere between balcony lifestyle photography, Japanese gravure-inspired editorial, and high-saturation outdoor fashion portraiture. The subject is often seated or leaning among branches, with leaves creating natural foreground frames and patterned shadows across skin and clothing.

# Set-Level Prompt Engineering Notes

## What Makes This Set Work

The photoset works because it combines **fresh green abundance** with **human intimacy**. The environment is not just a background; the leaves, branches, fruit, and sunlight actively shape the subject’s pose and framing.

Important prompt priorities:

1. Put the subject **inside the foliage**, not in front of it.
2. Use **branches and fig leaves as natural frames**.
3. Include **fruit props** clearly: watermelon, grapes, basket.
4. Use **dappled sunlight**, not flat studio light.
5. Keep the outfit white / light so it contrasts with saturated green.
6. Let hair look slightly messy and summer-natural.
7. Use shallow depth of field so the foliage becomes lush bokeh.
8. Include red watermelon for color contrast.

## Best Reusable Prompt Template

```text
a young woman with dark brown loose tied hair and soft bangs, wearing a white sleeveless soft draped neckline floral blouse with green and blue flower print and light cream shorts, seated under a dense fig tree on a sunny balcony garden ledge, surrounded by large green fig leaves, tree branches, wicker basket, green grapes, and a half watermelon with a spoon, dappled sunlight across her face and arms, saturated green foliage bokeh, fresh summer fruit editorial, natural outdoor daylight, shallow depth of field, vertical portrait photography, soft intimate gaze
```
