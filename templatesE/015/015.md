# Photoset 015 - White Birthday Princess Banquet Studio Portrait

**Language:** English
**Purpose:** reusable photoset prompt template for anime image generation.
**Reference rule:** reference photos define composition, pose, wardrobe language, lighting, props, and atmosphere only. They do not define the final character identity.

---

## 0. Global Photoset DNA

**Core identity:** white birthday banquet, silver tiara, white satin princess gown, cream cake, white roses, balloons, pale blue European interior, chandelier, soft high-key studio light.

**Wardrobe system:** white or ivory satin princess gown with thin straps or off-shoulder structure, delicate bow or strap detail, luminous satin skirt, silver tiara, small sparkling earrings and restrained jewelry.

**Scene system:** pale blue-gray European birthday room with white flowers, birthday cake, welcome sign, balloons, glass-panel doors, arched window, chandelier, white tablecloth, champagne glass and cream-colored floral arrangements.

**Lighting and color:** cool white high-key studio light, soft focus, clean skin highlights, bright satin reflections, white flowers with preserved detail, pale blue-gray background separation.

**Emotional tone:** quiet elegant birthday princess, sweet but restrained, refined ceremonial portrait.

**Continuity rule:** keep the same wardrobe family, prop language, lighting palette, and room atmosphere across the set while changing pose, crop, and camera distance for each image.
