## Global Set Identity

Before image-by-image analysis, the set has a clear unified identity:

- **Core Visual Theme:** soft domestic femininity, pastel loungewear, cute-kitten headband styling, airy apartment editorial.
- **Color Palette:** warm white, cream, soft beige, light wood, pale blush pink, tiny pastel floral accents.
- **Primary Scene Modes:**  
  1. white sofa / cushioned seating zone  
  2. bright kitchen counter with shelves and home props
- **Fashion Identity:** a fitted floral camisole-style romper / one-piece lounge garment with a soft pink-and-green micro floral print, delicate shoulder straps, delicate lace trim, and matching white lace ankle socks with ribbon bows.
- **Character Styling:** short dark bob hairstyle, softly curled ends, center-to-soft-side parting, natural glamour makeup, glossy lips, delicate necklace, plush white cat-ear headband with pink gingham bow.
- **Photographic Tone:** bright, clean, gentle, lifestyle-editorial, almost “Korean room-set portrait” in feel.
- **Lighting Mood:** diffuse daylight, likely window-supported, high key, low contrast, soft skin rendering, gentle background lift.
- **Prompt Utility:** each image can be repurposed into prompts focused on pose, furniture interaction, cute-home atmosphere, soft cat-girl styling, or pastel domestic editorial portraiture.

This image is shot in a **vertical portrait format** with a **medium-full body framing**.  
The camera appears to be set at approximately **chest to eye level relative to the seated subject**, creating a stable and flattering editorial perspective. The focal length impression is likely in the **50mm to 70mm full-frame equivalent range**, because there is only mild spatial compression, minimal distortion, and the proportions of face, torso, and legs remain natural.

The lens behavior suggests:
- no aggressive wide-angle stretch,
- a modest portrait compression,
- enough environmental inclusion to keep the shelving, bottles, vases, and countertop context readable.

This is important for prompting because the image is not a beauty close-up nor a distant environmental shot—it sits in the **clean lifestyle portrait middle zone**.

The lens still feels portrait-normal, approximately **50–70mm equivalent**, but the angle changes the emotional effect: instead of being upright and socially presented, the model becomes softer, more private, and more physically relaxed.

- emphasize the diagonals of bent legs,

The subject fills most of the frame diagonally. The sofa cushions form soft geometric planes around her, while her bent legs create a zigzag shape from lower left toward upper right. The body is arranged almost like a reclining S-curve.

The composition benefits from:

She reclines on her back / slight side recline with one leg bent and crossing over the other, torso angled slightly upward, and both hands gathered near the chest, touching a strap or clasping lightly. The pose is intimate and soft rather than performative.

## 6. Lighting
