# Template 249 - Frosted Evergreen Winter Portraits

## Set-Wide Visual Identity
Create a coherent winter photoset in a snow-covered evergreen forest. Frost and powdery snow densely coat the cedar-like branches, while distant trunks, rocks, and snowbanks dissolve into a cool blue-white background. Keep the visible branch placement and open negative space specific to each image; simplify only distant forest clutter that does not affect the composition.

The core outfit is an opaque ivory woven-knit poncho cape with a broad draped body, openwork geometric bands along the side edges, short fringe, occasional round wooden toggle details, and small fluffy white pom-pom accents near the upper cape. Under it is a muted mustard-ochre high-neck winter top with long fitted sleeves gathered into soft horizontal folds at the forearms. When visible, add oversized white teddy-fleece mittens and ivory micro-check winter trousers. Images 2, 3, 4, 6, and 7 use plain white plush earmuffs with a narrow white headband. Images 1 and 5 instead use an ivory cable-knit trapper bonnet with thick white faux-fur edging, long knitted ties, and fluffy pom-pom ends. Do not merge the bonnet and earmuffs in one image.

Use soft, high-key winter daylight with a cool blue ambient fill from the snow. Preserve the brighter upper-left or frontal daylight, faint cool shadows, restrained contrast, and nearly monochrome ivory, white, and ice-blue palette, with mustard sleeves as the only warm color accent. Render everything as a polished hand-drawn Japanese 2D anime illustration with visible clean black lineart, controlled cel-shaded separation, soft painted transitions, grouped hair shapes, and carefully drawn anime eyes. Never render a photograph, cosplay image, 3D model, or semi-realistic face.

Character references control only the selected character's canonical face, eyes, hair silhouette, bangs, permanent accessories, species traits, age impression, and body proportions. The current photoset image controls the outfit, hat or earmuffs, pose, hand visibility, camera, crop, scene, lighting, and palette. Never copy the reference person's face, hairstyle, or body type. No text, logo, watermark, or signature.
