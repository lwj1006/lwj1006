# Template 347 - Mint Blossom Garden

## Set-Wide Visual Identity

**Outfit:** Dress the selected character in a pale-mint sleeveless dress with a ribboned keyhole neckline, fitted waist, and white lace under-skirt, paired in designated shots with a cropped mint cardigan with ruffled cuffs and a straw hat trimmed with a mint ribbon and small daisies.

**Scene:** Keep a dense white-blossom garden with soft green leaves, pale branches, a low seat or garden path, and no artificial graphic overlays.

**Lighting and palette:** Use soft luminous backlight through blossoms, gentle green bounce, creamy highlights, and a fresh mint, white, straw, and leaf-green palette.

**Reference roles:** Character reference images define only the selected character's canonical identity and proportions. The final photoset image defines this shot's outfit, pose, hand contacts, camera, crop, set, props, light, and palette. Never copy the photoset model's face, hair, or body type.
