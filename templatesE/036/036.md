## Global Style DNA

This set is built around a refined **Chinese qipao / old courtyard portrait** aesthetic. The core visual identity is not bright festival color, but a muted and poetic palette: **sage green fabric, dusty rose orchids, dark wooden doors, burgundy-red plaques, black-green shadow, pale porcelain skin, and soft gray ambient light**.

The subject wears a slim, sleeveless or cap-sleeved **sage green qipao** with pink floral print. The dress is elegant rather than ornate: close-fitting silhouette, high Mandarin collar, pink piping, black frog-button closures, and delicate orchid motifs. Her hair is arranged in a low bun or smooth side-parted updo, decorated with large pink orchid flowers. The face direction is often profile, three-quarter profile, or downward gaze, creating a calm and distant emotional tone.

The background uses traditional architectural elements: dark red wooden panels, vertical Chinese calligraphy plaques, muted green-black doors, old courtyard depth, and classical wooden furniture. The lighting is subdued and cinematic, with soft falloff, shallow depth of field, and a slightly cool-green shadow grade.

**Reusable master prompt core:**

```text
elegant Chinese courtyard portrait, young woman in a sage green floral qipao with pink orchid pattern, pink orchid hair accessory in a low bun, soft porcelain skin, refined side profile, quiet melancholic expression, traditional dark wooden door with faded Chinese calligraphy plaques in the background, muted red and deep green architecture, cinematic shallow depth of field, soft diffused natural light, delicate hair strands crossing the cheek, vintage Chinese editorial photography, painterly realism, graceful hand gesture, muted sage green and dusty rose color palette, elegant restrained composition
```

# Image 01 — Upward Side Profile with Orchid Hairpiece

## Visual Summary

The first image is a vertical portrait focused on an elegant side profile. The subject stands or poses in front of a dark traditional wooden door with vertical red-brown plaques and blurred golden Chinese characters. Her body is turned sideways, while her face lifts upward toward the left, creating a gentle, aspirational line from shoulder to chin.

The most important feature is the **pink orchid cluster pinned into the low bun** at the back of the head. The orchid accessory repeats the floral motif printed on the qipao and turns the whole image into a coordinated botanical portrait. Her hands are folded around her opposite arm, holding a pink orchid near the forearm, giving the pose a quiet, self-contained elegance.

## Composition

The frame is a tight vertical half-body composition. The subject occupies the center-right of the image, while the background calligraphy panels fill the left and upper areas. The side profile creates a clean silhouette against the darker door panels.

The camera angle is slightly below or at shoulder height, looking up just enough to emphasize the neck line and lifted chin. The depth of field is shallow: the gold calligraphy behind her is readable as atmosphere rather than information.

## Pose and Gesture

The pose is restrained and classical. Her torso turns away from the camera, shoulders angled diagonally. The head tilts upward, eyes looking toward the upper-left area outside the frame. One arm crosses the body, while the other hand holds a flower near the elbow. The gesture feels protective, poetic, and slightly distant.

Key pose keywords:

```text
side profile, upward gaze, folded arms, hand holding orchid near forearm, elegant neck line, turned shoulder, quiet poised posture
```

## Styling and Wardrobe

The qipao is sage green with dusty pink floral print. It has a high collar, close-fitting torso, sleeveless cut, and pink trim around the arm opening. The fabric looks matte, soft, and slightly textured, avoiding glossy satin. This makes the image feel more literary and understated.

The orchid hairpiece is large enough to define the silhouette but not so large that it becomes costume-like.

## Lighting

The light is soft and low-contrast, likely natural shade. It falls gently across the face and shoulder, creating porcelain skin tones without strong highlights. The background is darker, so the face and shoulder stand out naturally.

Lighting keywords:

```text
soft diffused natural light, low contrast, cool shadow, gentle facial highlight, dark wooden background, muted cinematic tone
```

# Image 03 — Seated Qipao Pose with Orchid in Hand

## Visual Summary

The third image is a seated three-quarter portrait. The subject sits in front of calligraphy panels, looking downward to the left. The frame shows more of the qipao silhouette: fitted upper body, long skirt, floral pattern, and graceful seated posture. She holds an orchid in her extended left hand, making the flower a narrative object.

This image is useful for prompts involving **seated classical qipao composition**, **downcast expression**, and **quiet hand-object interaction**.

## Composition

The subject sits slightly right of center. Her body forms a curved diagonal from the raised knee down toward the extended hand. The background vertical calligraphy plaques create structure and height. The foreground wooden table edge at the bottom adds depth.

The camera angle is slightly elevated, looking down just enough to show the seated form and the qipao pattern.

## Pose and Gesture

She sits with one knee raised and the other leg folded or draped. The head tilts down. The left hand extends outward holding a pink orchid; the right arm rests along the body. The pose is elegant but not stiff. The emotional tone is introspective, almost like she is studying the flower.


## Styling and Wardrobe

# Image 05 — Standing with Paper Umbrella in Shadowed Courtyard

## Visual Summary

The fifth image moves outdoors or into a darker courtyard space. The subject stands in a full or three-quarter body pose, holding a pale paper umbrella. Her head tilts down, and the body is shown in profile. The atmosphere is cooler, darker, and more distant than the previous images.

This frame is excellent for prompts involving **qipao + paper umbrella**, **misty courtyard**, and **traditional Chinese silhouette**.

## Composition

The umbrella creates a large pale circular form near the top of the image, balancing the slim vertical figure below it. The subject stands slightly right of center. The dark background creates strong separation between the pale umbrella, face, arms, and sage green dress.

The camera is positioned at mid distance, allowing the whole pose and umbrella shape to read clearly.

## Pose and Gesture

She holds the umbrella upright with one hand. The other hand hangs gently downward in a soft, curved gesture. Her head is lowered, eyes cast down, creating a reserved and elegant mood. The pink orchid remains visible near the bun, tying this outdoor shot to the rest of the set.
