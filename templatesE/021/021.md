# Photoset 021 - Peach Qipao Clear Fan Hydrangea Portrait

**Language:** English
**Purpose:** reusable photoset prompt template for anime image generation.
**Reference rule:** reference photos define composition, pose, wardrobe language, lighting, props, and atmosphere only. They do not define the final character identity.

---

## 0. Global Photoset DNA

**Core identity:** warm peach qipao, transparent gold-dust round fan, pink hydrangeas, soft eastern portrait, gentle warm studio light.

**Wardrobe system:** peach or apricot qipao-inspired dress with refined collar and fitted clean silhouette, delicate floral detail and elegant hair accessories.

**Scene system:** soft peach studio with pink hydrangeas, transparent round fan with gold specks, pale flowers, warm fabric backdrop and restrained eastern props.

**Lighting and color:** warm soft portrait light, peach-pink highlights, translucent fan reflections, smooth face fill and shallow floral depth of field.

**Emotional tone:** gentle elegant eastern portrait, warm, feminine, quiet and polished.

**Continuity rule:** keep the same wardrobe family, prop language, lighting palette, and room atmosphere across the set while changing pose, crop, and camera distance for each image.
