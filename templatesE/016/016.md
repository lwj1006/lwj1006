**Purpose:** reusable photoset prompt template for anime image generation.
**Reference rule:** reference photos define composition, pose, wardrobe language, lighting, props, and atmosphere only. They do not define the final character identity.

## 0. Global Photoset DNA

**Core identity:** silver-gray rococo birthday banquet, pearl tiara, ornate white-silver gown, candlelit dessert table, crystal and lace details, pale aristocratic studio setting.

**Wardrobe system:** white and silver rococo princess dress with structured bodice, pearl or rhinestone trim, layered skirt, delicate gloves or jewelry, small crown-like headpiece.

**Emotional tone:** elegant birthday heroine, aristocratic but soft, refined celebration atmosphere.

**Continuity rule:** keep the same wardrobe family, prop language, lighting palette, and room atmosphere across the set while changing pose, crop, and camera distance for each image.
