# Photoset 017 - Soft Ivory Garden Window Floral Dress Portrait

**Language:** English
**Purpose:** reusable photoset prompt template for anime image generation.
**Reference rule:** reference photos define composition, pose, wardrobe language, lighting, props, and atmosphere only. They do not define the final character identity.

---

## 0. Global Photoset DNA

**Core identity:** soft ivory window garden, sheer floral dress, white flowers, garden room, lace curtains, gentle daylight and romantic pale botanical styling.

**Wardrobe system:** ivory or warm white floral dress with soft tulle, sheer sleeves or straps, lace trim, delicate flower accents and airy fabric layers.

**Scene system:** window-side garden room with white curtains, pale flowers, greenery, wicker or wooden furniture, soft fabric, vases and floral tabletop details.

**Lighting and color:** soft window daylight, warm ivory highlights, low contrast, gentle curtain diffusion, pale floral bloom and clean face readability.

**Emotional tone:** quiet romantic garden portrait, gentle and airy, intimate window-side atmosphere.

**Continuity rule:** keep the same wardrobe family, prop language, lighting palette, and room atmosphere across the set while changing pose, crop, and camera distance for each image.
