## 0. Set Overview

This set is built around a refined **blue hydrangea + modern qipao studio portrait** language. Compared with the previous courtyard qipao set, this series is cleaner, more editorial, and more studio-controlled. The major visual contrast is:

- **Cool blue textured wall**
- **White or pale blue qipao**
- **Blue and white hydrangea clusters**
- **Black ceramic vase**
- **Dark wood tea table**
- **Small celadon tea cup**
- **Soft porcelain skin**
- **Low bun with hydrangea hairpiece**
- **Minimal but elegant Chinese-modern styling**

The set has two sub-styles:

1. **Cool studio hydrangea portrait**  
   White sleeveless qipao, blue textured wall, black vase, hydrangea bouquet, quiet seated posture.

2. **Warm backlit qipao flower portrait**  
   Pale blue floral qipao, cream-white flower hairpiece, warm golden sidelight, soft old-room background.

The whole set feels like a mixture of **modern Chinese editorial portrait**, **minimal studio still life**, and **classical qipao beauty photography**. It is less ornate than the flower-wall sets and more controlled than the garden sets.

## 1. Global Visual DNA

### 1.1 Color Palette

The palette is dominated by cool, clean blue and white:

```text
powder blue, hydrangea blue, misty cyan wall, porcelain white, ivory white, pale celadon green, black ceramic, dark wood brown, soft warm gold highlight
```

The first three images use a highly controlled cool palette:

- Background: textured powder-blue wall
- Dress: white sleeveless qipao
- Flowers: blue and white hydrangeas
- Props: black rounded vase, celadon tea cup
- Surface: dark gray floor or dark brown table

The last three images shift to a warmer cinematic palette:

- Dress: pale sky-blue qipao with soft floral pattern
- Flowers: cream-white dahlias or peony-like blossoms
- Light: golden sidelight / sunset-like rim light
- Background: gray wall, dark furniture, soft blurred flower arrangement

### 1.2 Wardrobe Identity

The first half uses a **white sleeveless qipao**:

- High Mandarin collar
- Clean sleeveless cut
- Long fitted skirt
- Subtle ribbed or pleated vertical fabric texture
- Minimal decoration
- Elegant white shoes in the full-body shot
- Smooth, refined silhouette

The second half uses a **pale blue floral qipao**:

- Sleeveless or cap-sleeve qipao cut
- Soft translucent blue-green floral pattern
- Close-fitting silhouette
- White/cream floral hairpiece
- More romantic and cinematic lighting

### 1.3 Hair and Makeup

Hair is central to the identity:

- Smooth black or dark brown hair
- Center or near-center part
- Low bun at the nape or behind the ear
- Large blue hydrangea cluster on one side in the cool studio images
- Cream-white flower cluster on one side in the warm images
- A few loose strands near ear, cheek, or temple

Makeup:

- Porcelain skin
- Large clear eyes
- Soft pink blush
- Rosy natural lips
- Fine lashes
- Minimal eyeliner
- Doll-like but still photographic

### 1.4 Lighting

Two lighting systems appear:

**Cool studio lighting:**

```text
soft frontal studio light, even illumination, low shadow contrast, clean porcelain skin, cool blue background, gentle editorial clarity
```

**Warm cinematic side/backlight:**

```text
golden sidelight, rim light around hair, glowing flower highlights, dark gray background, warm skin highlights, shallow depth of field
```

### 1.5 Composition Style

The set alternates between:

- Full-body negative-space composition
- Table-side half-body still life composition
- Tight beauty close-up
- Side-profile standing portrait
- Extreme side-profile close-up
- Soft close-up with flower near face

The most reusable compositional motifs:

```text
large empty blue textured wall above subject, subject placed low in frame, oversized hydrangea bouquet beside body, black ceramic vase as anchor, hand near tea cup, flower held near cheek, profile looking left, warm rim light on hair
```

# 2. Image 01 — Wide Minimal Studio Portrait with Hydrangea Vase

## 2.1 Visual Summary

The first image is a wide vertical composition with a large amount of empty blue wall space. The subject sits low in the frame, leaning against a black ceramic vase overflowing with blue and white hydrangeas. She wears a clean white sleeveless qipao and white shoes. The composition feels like a fashion editorial poster because the negative space dominates the image.

The emotional tone is quiet, still, and slightly doll-like. The subject looks forward with a calm expression. The flowers and vase become almost sculptural props.

## 2.2 Composition

The key compositional feature is **negative space**. Around two-thirds of the upper image is just textured blue wall. This makes the subject and hydrangea bouquet feel small, refined, and carefully placed.

Composition notes:

- Vertical frame
- Subject seated near lower center-left
- Huge empty blue wall above
- Black vase on lower left
- Hydrangea bouquet spreads horizontally across the lower-left quadrant
- Subject’s white dress extends diagonally toward lower-right
- Blue petals scattered across floor
- Watermark/text in corners should be ignored or removed in prompt reuse

The body forms a gentle diagonal from head to feet. The vase acts as a visual counterweight to the extended skirt and legs.

## 2.3 Pose

The subject sits on the floor, leaning slightly back against or beside the flower vase. Her torso is upright but relaxed. One arm rests near the hip or skirt, while the other hand is placed softly on the lap. The legs extend toward the lower-right, creating an elegant reclining line.

Pose keywords:

```text
seated on floor, leaning beside large flower vase, long dress draped diagonally, relaxed elegant posture, calm forward gaze, one hand resting on lap, one hand near side
```

## 2.4 Wardrobe

The white qipao is minimal and modern:

- High collar
- Sleeveless
- Long skirt
- Subtle vertical ribbed fabric
- Clean ivory-white color
- No heavy embroidery
- Smooth sculptural silhouette

The simplicity of the outfit lets the blue flowers and wall dominate the set.

## 2.5 Props and Environment

Important props:

- Large black ceramic vase
- Blue hydrangeas
- White hydrangeas
- Scattered blue petals
- Textured blue wall
- Dark gray studio floor

The vase is important because it adds a dark mass to balance the pale subject. Without it, the image would become too soft and low-contrast.

## 2.6 Lighting

The light is soft and even. There are no aggressive shadows. The face is bright and clear, and the blue wall texture remains visible. The lighting resembles controlled studio light rather than natural sunlight.

Lighting prompt terms:

```text
soft even studio light, clean frontal illumination, gentle shadows, porcelain skin, cool blue editorial backdrop
```

## 2.7 Reusable Prompt


# 3. Image 02 — Table-Side Hydrangea Tea Portrait

## 3.1 Visual Summary

The second image is a half-body seated portrait beside a dark wooden round table. The hydrangea bouquet and black ceramic vase fill the left side, while the subject occupies the right side. She looks downward toward a small celadon tea cup and scattered blue petals.

This image is more intimate than the first. The tea cup introduces a delicate still-life element and creates a quiet domestic/classical mood.

## 3.2 Composition

The composition is split vertically:

- Left side: large hydrangea bouquet and black vase
- Right side: subject in white qipao
- Lower center: round dark wood tabletop
- Small celadon tea cup near the subject’s hand
- Blue textured background fills the entire rear plane

The subject’s gaze leads downward to the cup and petals. Her hand gesture is delicate, almost like she is arranging petals or about to lift the cup.

## 3.3 Pose

The subject is seated upright beside the table. Her head tilts down and left. One hand lightly touches blue petals on the tabletop. The other hand floats near the waist or rests on the dress. The pose is elegant and careful, like a still-life interaction rather than casual movement.

Pose keywords:

```text
seated beside round tea table, downward gaze, delicate fingers touching petals, one hand near waist, refined quiet posture
```

## 3.4 Wardrobe and Hair

The same white sleeveless qipao appears, emphasizing clean vertical lines. The blue hydrangea hairpiece is tucked into the low bun on the right side of the head, visually matching the bouquet.

The hair is smooth, with a neat center part and controlled bun. The effect is polished and formal.

## 3.5 Props

The celadon tea cup is small but important. It introduces a traditional Chinese tea aesthetic without making the image heavily historical. The cup color bridges the white dress and blue flowers.

Props prompt terms:

```text
small celadon tea cup, dark round wooden tea table, blue flower petals, black ceramic vase, blue and white hydrangea bouquet
```

## 3.6 Lighting

Lighting is soft but slightly more dimensional than the first image. The face is lit from front-left, with subtle shadow along the far side of the cheek and neck. The table surface catches a mild highlight.

## 3.7 Reusable Prompt

```text
half-body Chinese studio tea portrait, young woman in white sleeveless qipao with high Mandarin collar, seated beside a dark round wooden tea table, large black ceramic vase filled with blue and white hydrangeas on the left, small pale celadon tea cup on the tabletop, blue petals scattered near her delicate fingers, head tilted downward, calm introspective expression, low bun with blue hydrangea flower hairpiece, textured powder-blue wall background, porcelain skin, soft controlled studio lighting, shallow depth of field, elegant minimal Chinese editorial style
```
