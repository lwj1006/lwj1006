# Photoset 018 - Mint Qipao Tea Table and Autumn Berry Minimal Portrait

**Language:** English
**Purpose:** reusable photoset prompt template for anime image generation.
**Reference rule:** reference photos define composition, pose, wardrobe language, lighting, props, and atmosphere only. They do not define the final character identity.

---

## 0. Global Photoset DNA

**Core identity:** two-part eastern portrait set: mint sleeveless qipao tea-table scene, then minimal white background with autumn berry branches and ivory long dress.

**Wardrobe system:** front half uses pale mint sleeveless qipao with elegant fitted silhouette and low floral hair arrangement; back half uses ivory loose long dress with soft drape and restrained classical styling.

**Scene system:** dark carved wooden tea table with celadon ceramics, pale pink-white flowers, gray beige wall and rain-glass window; later a minimal white studio with orange berry branches, dry leaves and large negative space.

**Lighting and color:** quiet low-saturation eastern soft light, gentle window diffusion, porcelain ceramic highlights, then high-key white studio light with autumn branch shadows.

**Emotional tone:** calm literary eastern portrait, restrained, elegant, slow and contemplative.

**Continuity rule:** keep the same wardrobe family, prop language, lighting palette, and room atmosphere across the set while changing pose, crop, and camera distance for each image.
